# Painted adventure artwork

These six illustrations were generated for the 0.6.0 active-table redesign.
They are self-contained local PNG assets; the app has no image service calls.

- `lighthouse.png`: coastal lighthouse, beacon, keeper's cottage and rescue boat.
- `library.png`: midnight archive, shelves, desk lamp and evidence.
- `lucy.png`: terrier with a blue ribbon, orchard, shed and footbridge.
- `lantern-empty.png`, `oil-flask.png`, `lantern-lit.png`: transparent item
  illustrations for the first lighthouse recipe.

Art direction: detailed hand-painted gouache/watercolor, warm paper, aged
brass, amber and sea teal; inviting mystery rather than horror. Covers have
no embedded titles. Card labels and clue codes are real interface text,
not generated lettering. Item silhouettes stay within their transparent
canvas. The original vector illustrations remain under `art/story/`.
